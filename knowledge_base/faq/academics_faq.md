# Academics & Attendance Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. How do I calculate my attendance percentage?
- Formula: $(\text{Classes Attended} + \text{Approved Duty Leaves}) \div \text{Total Classes Delivered} \times 100$.
- Example: 30 attended lectures + 3 approved Duty Leaves out of 44 delivered classes = `33 / 44 × 100 = 75.0%`.
- Always track **subject-wise** attendance live on **UMS (`ums.lpu.in`)** or **LPU Touch**.

### Q2. What is the minimum attendance required, and what happens if I fall short?
- Students generally need **at least 75% attendance per individual course** to sit for the **End-Term Examination (ETE)** (some frameworks state 80% expected aggregate with a 5% relaxation down to 75%).
- Falling short by the end of classes (e.g., **Dec 11, 2026** for Autumn Term 2026–27) leads to **detention** in that course: you cannot write the ETE and must repeat/clear the course as a backlog under UMS rules.
- *Verify exact medical condonation ceilings or statutory council rules (e.g., Pharmacy/Law/Architecture) in UMS -> Academic Ordinances.*

### Q3. How do I apply for Duty Leave (DL) or correct a wrong attendance mark?
- **Duty Leave (DL):** Applied/endorsed via the organizing faculty/DSW/Placement Cell for authorized university events, hackathons, sports, or placement drives, and tracked inside UMS.
- **Wrong Faculty Marking:** Report immediately to the course faculty and raise a ticket in **UMS -> RMS** before the attendance correction deadline closes.

### Q4. Where and when do I register for Courses, Open Minors, or Electives?
- All registrations take place inside **UMS -> Courses / Registration**.
- For **Autumn Term 2026–27** regular students, **Advance Registration for Spring Term Electives & Minors** is officially scheduled on **Saturday, November 14, 2026**. An Open/Interdisciplinary Minor consists of **4 cohesive courses** across 70+ available tracks.
- For **add/drop** or **course withdrawal** windows, check active **UMS E-Notices** at the start of the semester (*exact cutoff hours vary per term*).

### Q5. Can I get academic credit for NPTEL, SWAYAM, or Coursera MOOCs?
- Yes, LPU officially supports credit/content mapping for approved **MOOCs (NPTEL, SWAYAM, Coursera)** and hackathon achievements per departmental circulars posted in UMS (`lpu.in/academics/curriculum-innovations.php`).
