# Hostel, Mess & Campus Living FAQ — LPU

### Q1. What are the hostel types and fees for 2026–2027?
- **Standard Air-Cooled:** ₹72,000 (5/6/7/8/4x2-str) to ₹1,10,700 (2-str) with 250–400 free electricity units.
- **Standard AC:** ₹81,000 (5/6/7/8-str) to ₹1,46,700 (1-str) with 350–1,000 free units.
- **Studio Apartments AC:** ₹99,000 (4-str) to ₹1,80,000 (1-str) with 500–1,000 free units.

### Q2. Which electrical appliances are allowed inside LPU hostels?
- **Permitted in all hostel rooms:** Kettle, Iron, Trimmer, Hair Dryer, Hair Straightener.
- **Permitted ONLY in Studio Apartments:** Induction Plate.
- Unauthorized cooking stoves, heaters, alcohol, tobacco, and drugs are strictly prohibited.

### Q3. How do I apply for hostel leave or night-out permission?
- Hostel leave / gate passes are submitted digitally through **UMS / LPU Touch / Pro Bricks**, triggering automated **parental notification/verification** and turnstile smart-card tracking (`lpu.in/student-services/residence.php`, `security.php`). Check your block circular in UMS for exact daily gate entry timings.

### Q4. How many meals does the hostel mess serve and can I order outside food?
- The centralized FSSAI-certified hostel mess serves **4 meals per day** (50+ daily menu items) managed via **Pro Bricks**. Students can also eat at **UniMall** (CCD, Domino's, La Pino'z, Lovely Sweets) and multi-cuisine food kiosks campus-wide.
