# Finance, Fee Payment, Scholarships & Refunds FAQ — LPU

### Q1. How do I pay my semester fees and when is the deadline?
- Continuing students pay online via **UMS (`ums.lpu.in`) -> Fee Details / Online Payment** (or **LPU Touch**) using UPI, Net Banking, Cards, or approved bank transfer.
- Official deadline rule: **Each successive semester's fee must be paid before the teaching period of the preceding semester ends** (e.g., before Term-I classes close on Dec 11, 2026 — confirm exact fine-free date inside UMS E-Notice).

### Q2. What is the 2026 tuition and examination fee for B.Tech CSE, MBA, and BBA?
- Verified for 2026 intake Regular programmes (`GetProgramFeeDetail`): **B.Tech. CSE (`P132`)** = **₹1,60,000/sem**; **MBA (`P371-NNAF`)** = **₹2,20,000/sem**; **BBA (`P321`)** = **₹80,000/sem**. Standard **Examination Fee** = **₹5,000/sem** (1st-sem exam fee may be paid with 2nd-sem fee or earlier). Check UMS Fee Details for your specific cohort/programme.

### Q3. How do scholarships work and can LPU help with education loans?
- Scholarships cover **Brackets 1–4** by qualifying marks (80%–98%+ after 10+2; 70%–95%+ after graduation), **LPUNEST** (up to 50% in Phase V B.Tech CSE), **LPU RISE** (100% fee + 4-seater non-AC hostel), national tests (JEE/CUET/CAT/GATE), sports, and Financial Aid.
- **Student Loan Assistance Cell:** `+91-1824-444583`, `studentloanassistance@lpu.co.in` (bank loans + collateral-free NBFC loans up to ₹30 Lakh + Easy EMI).

### Q4. How do I apply for or check the status of a Fee Refund?
- Submit the Refund Application Form and track real-time status on **LPUAdmit (`https://admission.lpu.in/`) -> Post Admission Services**.
- Prospectus, Application, Processing (₹5,000 migration processing), Entrance, Late, and Transfer fees are non-refundable. Term fee deductions increase by application date (10% -> 20% -> 50% -> No Refund after mid-September in the published 2025–2026 schedule). Education-loan refunds require a bank NOC to refund directly to the student instead of the lending bank.
