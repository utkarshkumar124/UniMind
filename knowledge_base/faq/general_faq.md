# General LPU Student Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Direct Answer
This FAQ covers the most common general student questions at Lovely Professional University: accessing UMS/MyClass/LPU Live, contacting university departments, requesting official certificates, campus medical emergencies, and filing grievances.

### Q1. How do I access my student portal and online classes?
- Log in to **UMS (`https://ums.lpu.in/`)** or download the **LPU Touch** Android app (`ums.lovely.university`) with your Registration Number and password for attendance, timetable, fee details, exam results, E-Notices, and RMS.
- Use **MyClass (`https://myclass.lpu.in/`)** with UMS credentials for online live classes, and **LPU Live (`https://lpulive.lpu.in/`)** for real-time faculty/class chat groups.

### Q2. How do I contact the relevant department or escalate an academic/administrative issue?
- **Current Students (Primary Path):** Submit an online ticket under **UMS -> Relationship Management System (RMS)** routed to the exact department, or contact your assigned Faculty Mentor / **Division of Student Relationship (DSR)** at **Block 32, Room No. 100** (`+91-1824-520150`, `dsr@lpu.co.in`).
- **General / Admissions Helpline:** `+91-1824-517000`, `+91-1824-404404`, WhatsApp `+91-98525-69000`, `info@lpu.co.in`.
- **Formal Grievance Portal:** `https://www.lpu.in/student-grievance-redressal.php` (SGRC Chairperson Dr. Lovi Raj Gupta; Ombudsperson Dr. C.L. Chandan).
- **Registrar / HEI Feedback:** `registrar@lpu.co.in`, `+91-1824-510274`, `+91-9915020408`.

### Q3. How do I get a Bonafide, Transcript, Migration, or Character Certificate?
- Apply through the official **UMS Certificate Portal (`https://ums.lpu.in/lpuums/LoginNew.aspx?loginPage=ExtCert`)** (listed on `lpu.in` as *Apply Certificate*) or inside **UMS**, clear any library/hostel/fee no-dues, and track delivery online. For Education Loan Fee Letters, email `studentloanassistance@lpu.co.in` (`+91-1824-444583`).

### Q4. Where is the Central Library located and what are its hours?
- The **Central Library** is in **Block-37**, alongside 12 branch libraries. **Book Circulation Service runs 8:00 AM – 9:00 PM** and **Documentation Service runs 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM**; **KOHA OPAC** is online **24x7x365** (`Mapmyaccess` for remote e-journals/IEEE/Springer/NDL; `central.library@lpu.co.in`).

### Q5. Who do I call for a medical emergency or psychological counselling?
- **24x7 Uni Health Centre & Ambulance:** Call **`01824-501227`** (`01824-444079`, `9780036453`, `7508182840`). OPD doctor consultation is **free**; 24-hour Chemist Shop is on site (Ext. `4068`).
- **24x7 Free Confidential Psychological Counselling:** Contact **Dr. Vishal Sharma (Nodal Officer, Student Well-Being, DSR)** at `01824-520150` / `dsr@lpu.co.in` or the Uni Health Centre Psychological OPD.
