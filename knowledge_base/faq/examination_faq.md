# Examination & Grading Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. How do I apply for a reappear examination, and when are 2026–27 reappears held?
- Log in to **UMS (`ums.lpu.in`) -> Examination -> Reappear Registration**, select your pending course(s), and pay the prescribed reappear fee online during the E-Notice window.
- **Verified Autumn Term 2026–2027 Dates:** Last date to apply is **Saturday, August 29, 2026**; Reappear Exams commence **Saturday, September 05, 2026**.
- *Note: Exact per-paper reappear fees are displayed directly on UMS at registration and are not listed publicly outside UMS.*

### Q2. How do I calculate TGPA and CGPA?
- Multiply each course's **credits** by its awarded **10-point grade point**, sum across all courses, and divide by total credits:
  $$\text{TGPA/CGPA} = \frac{\sum (\text{Credits} \times \text{Grade Point})}{\sum \text{Credits}}$$
- Example: 4 credits (`9` GP) + 4 credits (`8` GP) + 2 credits (`10` GP) = `(36 + 32 + 20) / 10 = 8.80`.

### Q3. How do I check my results and when do Autumn Term 2026–27 results release?
- Check results on **UMS (`ums.lpu.in`) -> Examination / Grade Sheet** or **LPU Touch**.
- **Verified Term-I 2026–27 Schedule:** ETE runs **Dec 14–30, 2026**; Results publish **Tuesday, January 12, 2027**; Physical Scrutiny is **January 14 & January 16, 2027**; Online Grade Sheet releases **Wednesday, January 27, 2027**.

### Q4. What should I do if my CA, MTT, or ETE marks are incorrect?
- **CA marks:** Contact your course faculty immediately and log an **UMS RMS** request before CA locks prior to ETE.
- **MTT / ETE marks:** Apply via UMS for **Physical Answer Booklet Scrutiny** (scheduled **Jan 14 & 16, 2027** for Term-I) to inspect your evaluated script for totalling/unmarked errors, and if needed submit an **online Re-evaluation application** on UMS (`lpu.in/academics/assessment-and-evaluation.php`).

### Q5. What happens if I am booked under Unfair Means (UMC) in an exam?
- Carrying phones, smartwatches, chits, or talking/copying triggers an official **UMC report** and Committee hearing. Penalties include **cancellation of the exam paper (`'F'` grade)**, **potential debarment from future exams**, and **loss of scholarship eligibility**.
