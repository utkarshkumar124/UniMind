# Campus Placements & Division of Career Services — LPU

## Direct Answer
Placements at LPU are managed by the **Division of Career Services** (`+91-1824-444500`, `+91-9915020421`, `placement@lpu.co.in`) through the **UMS Placement Module**. Per official `lpu.in/placements.php` disclosures for **Batches 2021 to 2026**, **2,225+ recruiters** hired LPU students, generating **6,000+ offers from Fortune 500 companies**, **2,000+ students securing packages between ₹10 Lakh and ₹2.5 Crore**, and an average salary of **₹10.23 Lakh** for the **top 25% placed students**.

## 1. Verified Placement Statistics (Batches 2021–2026)
Verified directly from `https://www.lpu.in/placements.php`:

- **Recognitions:** National Employability Award 2024; highest North India campus selections with Bosch, Cognizant, and Capgemini.

## 2. Placement Process & Eligibility Overview
1. **Registration:** Final-year/pre-final-year students enroll in Placement Services via **UMS -> Placements** and choose career pathways (**Corporate Jobs, Government Exams, Higher Studies, Entrepreneurship, Study Overseas**).
2. **Company-Specific Eligibility Filters:** Each recruiting company sets its own criteria (minimum **CGPA / 10th / 12th %**, **active backlog / zero-backlog rule**, and branch eligibility) published inside UMS Placement Drive Notices.
   > **Verification Caveat:** Universal placement policy documents (such as dream/super-dream offer cap rules and drive attendance rules) are hosted inside **UMS -> Division of Career Services Policy Portal** (**Not published publicly on `lpu.in`** — review them inside UMS upon placement registration).
3. **Contact:** `placement@lpu.co.in` | `+91-1824-444500`, `+91-9915020421`.
