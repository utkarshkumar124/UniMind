# Career Services & Professional Enhancement Training — LPU

## Direct Answer
LPU's **Centre for Professional Enhancement (CPE)** delivers structured **PEP (Placement Enhancement Programme)** courses inside and beyond the regular timetable covering **Soft Skills, Verbal Ability, Analytical/Quantitative Aptitude, Technical/Coding readiness, Foreign Languages (German, French, Japanese, Spanish, Mandarin)**, mock interviews, and **20,000+ annual industry-grade assessments** via **SHL (AMCAT), AON (CoCubes), eLitmus, Mercer Mettl, Examly, Hitbullseye, MyPerfectice, and OAS**.

## 1. Key CPE Components (Verified)
Verified from `https://www.lpu.in/campus-life/career-enhancement-initiatives.php`:
- **Entry-Level Diagnostic Test:** Administered after admission to benchmark quantitative & verbal proficiency and route students into levelled training tracks.
- **4 Core Training Departments:**
  1. **Department of Soft Skills:** Personality development, workplace conduct, resume/CV branding, Group Discussions (GD), and Mock Personal Interviews.
  2. **Department of Verbal Ability:** LSRW skills benchmarked with CEFR levels, AI-enabled virtual language labs.
  3. **Department of Analytical Skills:** Cognitive aptitude, quantitative reasoning, higher-order problem solving.
  4. **Department of Foreign Languages:** Certificate courses in German, French, Japanese, Spanish, Mandarin, etc.
- **Practice & Assessment Platforms:**
  - **Practice:** **Examly**, **Hitbullseye**, **MyPerfectice**, **OAS** (company-specific mock tests).
  - **Assessments:** **SHL (AMCAT)**, **AON (CoCubes)**, **eLitmus**, **Mercer Mettl**.
- **Competitive Exam Preparation:** Dedicated mentoring for **GATE, GRE, GMAT, UPSC, SSC, UGC-NET, AFCAT, IELTS, and TOEFL** (**2,100+ qualifiers** reported on official page; **1,500+ annual capacity-building events**; **50+ industry training partners**).
