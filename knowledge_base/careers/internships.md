# Internships, Live Projects & Industry Immersion — LPU

## Direct Answer
LPU integrates **6-month / summer industry internships**, **live industry projects**, **work-integrated programmes**, and **part-time on-campus jobs** directly into its Outcome-Based curriculum. Students secure Pre-Placement Offers (PPOs) and semester-long internships via the **Division of Career Services** and academic school industry-interface cells (for example, documented 6-month internships at ServiceNow, Amazon, and OpenText).

## 1. Internship & Experiential Pathways (Verified)
- **Full-Semester / 6-Month Corporate Internships:** Final-year UG/PG students (e.g., B.Tech., MBA, Architecture, Design) can undertake accredited 6-month internships / On-the-Job Training (OJT) via UMS placement/internship approvals, frequently converting into full-time PPOs.
- **Summer Training & Live Projects (`lpu.in/academics/live-projects.php`):** Credited curriculum modules pairing student teams with industry problem statements, incubation ventures, or NGO/community projects.
- **Industry Immersion & Collaborations:** Joint programmes and specialized labs supported by industry partners (Google, Microsoft, IBM, Cisco, Amazon, KPMG, SHRM, Bosch).
- **On-Campus Work Opportunities:** Students can apply for compensated **On-Campus Jobs** (`lpu.in/campus-life/on-campus-jobs.php`) managed by DSW/HR.
