# Residential Facilities (Hostels & Apartments) — LPU

## Direct Answer
LPU provides on-campus residential accommodation across three tiers—**Standard Air-Cooled**, **Standard AC**, and **Studio Apartments AC**—for both boys and girls, ranging from **₹72,000 to ₹1,80,000** for the **2026–2027 academic session** depending on room type and occupancy (1-seater to 8-seater/dormitory), each including a fixed **free annual electricity unit allowance**. Residential services, leave requests, mess, laundry, and gym access are managed digitally via **UMS** and the **Pro Bricks** app.

## 2. Allowed & Prohibited Electrical Appliances (Verified)
- **Allowed Electrical Gadgets Inside Hostels:**
  - Electric **Kettle**
  - **Iron**
  - **Trimmer**
  - **Hair Dryer**
  - **Hair Straightener**
  - **Induction Plate:** Permitted **ONLY inside Apartments** (prohibited in Standard Hostel rooms).
- **Strict Prohibition:** Unauthorized high-wattage appliances (room heaters, immersion rods, unauthorized cooking stoves in standard rooms, etc.), alcohol, drugs, tobacco/smoking, and weapons are strictly prohibited.

## 3. Security, Turnstile Entry, Leave & Room Rules
- **Security & Surveillance:** Separate gated residential blocks for boys and girls (`lady security staff` for girls' hostels), **400+ residential security personnel**, **7,400+ campus CCTV cameras**, **biometric/smart-card turnstile gates** tracking all hostel entry and exit.
- **Digital Hostel & Leave Workflow:** Hostel leave/night-out requests are applied digitally via **UMS / Pro Bricks**, with automated **parental notification/approval** tracking.
- **Room/Bed Allotment:** Residents must occupy only their allotted room and bed; swapping rooms or shifting beds without written Warden / Residential Services approval is prohibited.
- **Hostel Gate Timings:** Exact evening entry curfew times are notified per hostel block in the **Hostel Residents Handbook** and UMS circulars (**Not verified from an official public `lpu.in` page** — confirm with your Hostel Warden or UMS Residential Services).

## 4. In-Hostel Amenities & Laundry
- Wi-Fi, attached/common washrooms (with geysers), study table/chair/almirah/cot (mattress/bedding usually brought or bought by student), 24x7 power backup, RO drinking water, tuck shops, and paid/app-managed **Laundry service** via **Pro Bricks**.
