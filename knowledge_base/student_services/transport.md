# Transport Facilities & Fee Structure — LPU

## Direct Answer
LPU operates an extensive dedicated **bus fleet** for day scholars across Punjab (Jalandhar, Phagwara/nearby, Ludhiana, Amritsar, Kapurthala, Hoshiarpur, Adampur, Mukerian, Nawanshahr) with **2026–2027 annual Non-AC fees starting from ₹25,000** up to **₹70,000** depending on city and distance. Inside the campus, students move via **electric rickshaws, solar-powered carts, and bicycles**.

## 2. Intra-Campus Mobility
- Green campus **electric rickshaws**, **solar-powered carts**, and **bicycles** circulate along main campus roads connecting academic blocks, Block-37 Central Library, Uni Health Centre, and residential zones.
- Dedicated mobility vehicles and wheelchairs are provided for persons with disabilities.
