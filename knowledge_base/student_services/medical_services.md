# Medical Services & Uni Health Centre — LPU

## Direct Answer
LPU operates an in-campus **24x7 Uni Health Centre (Uni Hospital)** staffed by **40+ doctors and paramedics**, featuring **free OPD doctor consultation** for students and staff, **20+ IPD inpatient beds** (with separate male, female, and isolation wards), a **24-hour Chemist Shop** with subsidized medicines, diagnostic **Medical Laboratory**, **6 round-the-clock ambulances**, and tie-ups with **25+ specialized hospitals** across Jalandhar, Phagwara, and Ludhiana.

## 2. Clinical Facilities Available
- **OPD Departments (Zero Consultation Charge):** General Medicine, General Surgery, Eye Consultation & Testing, Dental Consultation & Procedures, Gynaecology Consultation, Skin (Dermatology) Consultation, Ayurvedic Consultation, **Psychological Consultation**, and Physiotherapy Clinic.
- **IPD & Emergency Care:** Separate **Male Ward**, **Female Ward**, **Male Isolation Ward**, **Female Isolation Ward**, ECG room, and immediate stabilization before referral to empaneled specialty hospitals (25+ hospitals in Phagwara, Jalandhar, Ludhiana).
- **Diagnostics & Pharmacy:** Pathologist-supervised medical lab; 24x7 subsidized Chemist Shop (dispenses medicines against doctor prescriptions).

## 3. Frequently Asked Questions
- **How much does an OPD check-up cost?**
  **₹0 consultation charge** for OPD visits; students only pay for prescribed medicines (at subsidized chemist rates) and recommended diagnostic lab tests.
- **How do I get an ambulance on campus at night?**
  Call **`01824-501227`** (or `01824-444079` / `9780036453`) — **6 ambulances** operate **24x7** on campus.
