# Student Welfare, Counselling & Support Services — LPU

## Direct Answer
Student support at LPU is coordinated by the **Division of Student Relationship (DSR)** (`Block 32, Room No. 100`, `+91-1824-520150`, `dsr@lpu.co.in`) and the **Division of Student Welfare (DSW)** (`+91-1824-444500`, `director.dcs@lpu.co.in`). Services include **1-on-1 Faculty Mentoring**, remedial academics, **free confidential 24x7 psychological counselling**, crisis response, international student integration, and part-time **On-Campus Jobs** ("Earn While You Learn").

## 1. Counselling & Mental Well-Being Services (Verified)
Verified from `https://www.lpu.in/academics/mentoring-advising.php` and `healthcare.php`:
- **Free & Confidential Psychologists:** Counselling services are available **round the clock** (`24x7`) providing **free and confidential sessions** by qualified psychologists for emotional well-being, stress, adjustment, or personal crises.
- **Nodal Officer for Student Well-Being, Crisis Response & Compliance:** **Dr. Vishal Sharma** (`dsr@lpu.co.in`, `+91-1824-520150`, **Block 32, Room 100**).
- **Holistic Wellness Initiatives:** Campus Yoga, Meditation, and Pranayama programmes (19,800+ participants in 2024–25) plus Uni Health Centre psychological OPD.

## 2. Mentoring & Academic Advising
- **Dedicated Faculty Mentor:** Assigned to every student from Term-I to monitor attendance, grades, personal goals, and parent communication.
- **Tailored Remedial Support:** Additional Tutorial Classes, Remedial Sessions, and Self-Learning Materials for challenging courses.

## 3. On-Campus Part-Time Jobs ("Earn While You Learn") & Lost-and-Found
- **On-Campus Jobs (`lpu.in/campus-life/on-campus-jobs.php`):** Eligible students can apply via UMS/DSW for part-time campus support roles in libraries, laboratories, event operations, administrative offices, and peer tutoring alongside classes.
- **Lost & Found / Security Assistance:** Handled through the Campus Security Control Room & Block Security Desks supported by 7,400+ CCTVs (`lpu.in/student-services/security.php`).
