# Sports, Fitness Centres & Gym Facilities — LPU

## Direct Answer
LPU features an Olympic-grade **Indoor Sports Stadium**, **Indoor Swimming Pool**, **10m Shooting Arena**, **Badminton Courts**, **Boxing Ring**, **Football & Rugby Fields**, **Kabaddi Ground**, **Box Cricket**, **Chess Strategy Zone**, and **Fitness Centres / Gyms** across residential and UniMall complexes. Gym and indoor sports memberships are managed via **UMS / Pro Bricks**.

## 1. Sports Infrastructure & Achievements (Verified)
- **Indoor & Specialized Arenas:** Indoor Sports Stadium, Indoor Swimming Pool, Shooting Arena, Badminton Courts, Boxing Ring, Table Tennis, Chess Zone.
- **Outdoor Fields:** Football Field, Rugby Field, Hockey Ground, Cricket / Box Cricket, Basketball, Volleyball, Kabaddi Ground, Track & Field.
- **Sporting Pedigree (`lpu.in/campus-life/sports.php`):**
  - 1st Runner-up in **MAKA Trophy 2024** and **1st Runner-up (3 consecutive years) in Khelo India University Games**.
  - **3,100+ student participations** and **400+ medals** in national & international sports events, with LPU athletes including **Neeraj Chopra**, **Mirabai Chanu**, **Elavenil Valarivan**, **Dhiraj Bommadevara**, and **Balraj Panwar** representing India at the Olympics, Asian Games, and World Championships.

## 2. Gym & Paid Sports Registration
- Students register for **Gym** or **Indoor Sports / Swimming** facilities through **UMS -> Residential / Facility Registration** and access them via **Pro Bricks** / Sports ID cards.
- Exact per-term optional Gym / Indoor Sports add-on charges vary by plan tier and are visible during UMS booking (**Not verified as a fixed single fee on public `lpu.in` pages**; note `2025-26 Refund Example` confirms Gym & Indoor Sports Facility carry separate term fees with `₹200` base deduction in early refund tiers).
