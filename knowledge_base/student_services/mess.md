# Mess & Campus Dining Facilities — LPU

## Direct Answer
Hostel residents at LPU can opt for the centralized **Campus Mess** serving **4 meals per day** (Breakfast, Lunch, Evening Tea/Snacks, and Dinner) prepared across **FSSAI-certified** kitchens with **50+ daily menu items** and regional diversity. Mess bookings, meal tracking, and feedback are managed digitally through **UMS** and the **Pro Bricks** app.

## 1. Mess Standards & Operations (Verified)
Verified from `https://www.lpu.in/student-services/residence.php`:
- **4 Meals Daily:** Structured 4-meal plan covering Breakfast, Lunch, Snacks/Evening Tea, and Dinner.
- **Menu Variety:** **50+ items daily** catering to North Indian, South Indian, and pan-Indian / diverse student preferences.
- **Food Safety & Hygiene:** **FSSAI-certified** modern kitchens featuring automated chapati-making/cooking equipment and regular quality inspections.
- **Digital Management:** Meal plans, mess rebates/preferences, and daily operations run via the **Pro Bricks** platform and **UMS -> Residential Service**.

## 2. Additional Campus Dining Options
Outside the hostel mess, students have access to **UniMall food courts**, residential-block kiosks, and branded outlets (`lpu.in/student-services/shopping-dining.php`) including **Café Coffee Day, Domino's Pizza, La Pino'z Pizza, Lovely Sweets**, plus South Indian, North Indian, African, and Bhutanese food kiosks across campus.

## 3. Mess Fee Note
- **Verification Caveat:** Exact 2026–2027 optional Mess plan fees (standard vs a-la-carte / yearly fee brackets) are displayed inside the student's **LPUAdmit / UMS Residential Booking** portal (**Not printed directly in the public `residence.php` fee table**). Always confirm live mess fee slabs in UMS before opting in.
