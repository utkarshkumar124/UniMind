# Central Library & Branch Libraries — LPU

## Direct Answer
LPU's **Central Library** is located in **Block-37** (spanning 10,000+ sq.mt.) and is supported by **12 branch libraries** across campus. It holds **5+ lakh books & e-books** (including 3+ lakh e-books), **60,000+ e-journals**, **2,000+ newspapers**, and **800+ magazines**, runs on **KOHA ILMS** with **24x7x365 OPAC**, and offers off-campus digital database access via **Mapmyaccess**. Standard **Book Circulation Service** runs **8:00 AM to 9:00 PM**.

## 1. Key Service Timings & Contact
- **Location:** **Block-37** (Central Library) + 12 departmental/branch libraries.
- **Book Circulation (Issue/Return) Service:** **8:00 AM to 9:00 PM**.
- **Documentation Service:** **9:00 AM to 5:00 PM**.
- **Web OPAC Catalogue & Automated Kiosks:** **24x7x365** (`KOHA` Integrated Library Management System with RFID & Barcode security gates + self-check kiosks).
- **Exam-Period Extended Reading Hours:** Extended night reading-hall hours before/during MTT and ETE are announced separately via **UMS E-Notice**.
- **Library Support & Inter-Library Loan (ILL):** `central.library@lpu.co.in`

## 2. Digital E-Resources & Remote Access
- **Remote Off-Campus Login:** Accessed via **Mapmyaccess** (`UMS -> Library` / Mapmyaccess portal).
- **Subscribed Databases & Repositories:** **IEEE, Springer, ProQuest, Manupatra, DELNET, JSTOR, Shodhganga, INFLIBNET, National Digital Library of India (NDL)**, and institutional research repositories.
- **Specialized Sections:** Dedicated Research Commons, Computer Access Terminals, Periodicals & Newspapers section, and Inter-Library Loan (DELNET).
