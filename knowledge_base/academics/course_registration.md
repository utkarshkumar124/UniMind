# Course Registration, Electives & Add/Drop Guide

## Direct Answer
All course, elective, and minor registrations at LPU are executed online through the **University Management System (UMS -> Courses / Registration module)**. For the 2026–2027 session, **Advance Registration for Electives and Minors for Spring Term (Term-II)** is officially scheduled for **Saturday, November 14, 2026**.

## Registration Components

### 1. Core vs Elective vs Minor Registration
- **Core Courses:** Mandatory foundation and discipline core courses prescribed for your semester and admission batch (normally auto-registered / pre-mapped in UMS once semester fee and promotion rules are satisfied).
- **Discipline Electives & Generic Electives:** Chosen during the designated UMS elective registration window to build domain specialization or broad academic exposure.
- **Open Minor / Engineering Minor / Interdisciplinary Minor:** A cohesive sequence of **four elective courses** allowing students to specialize in an adjacent engineering track or an unrelated interdisciplinary area (70+ official minors listed on `lpu.in/academics/Interdisciplinary-minors.php`).

### 2. Official Registration Timeline (2026–2027 Verified Date)
- **Spring Term-II Advance Elective / Minor Registration:** **November 14, 2026** (for Autumn Term-I regular students transitioning to Spring Term-II).
- All term-by-term open/close windows for **course registration, section change, backlog registration, and add/drop** are published via **UMS E-Notice** (`ums.lpu.in`).

### 3. Add/Drop & Course Withdrawal Rules
- **Verify Before Acting:** Exact number of add/drop days after term commencement, maximum allowed credit load per semester, and conditions for course withdrawal (W grade) are **Not verified from an official public PDF** on `lpu.in` because those operational circulars are posted per semester on **UMS -> E-Notice / Relationship Management System (RMS)** and Academic Regulations.
- **Practical Guidance:** Monitor UMS E-Notices immediately during the first 1–2 weeks of the semester if you need an elective swap or backlog registration, as late registration incurs late fees or closes strictly once attendance locks in.
