# Academic Progression, Promotion Rules & Migration

## Direct Answer
Students progress semester-by-semester by earning required course credits and maintaining the minimum CGPA and attendance standards prescribed for their programme batch. Students transferring **into LPU from another accredited university (Advanced Standing / Migration)** must have **zero pending backlogs/reappears**, pay a non-refundable **₹5,000 processing fee** + **₹5,000 migration/transfer fee** upon approval, and apply **by December for Even Term** or **before admission closing for Odd Term**.

## 1. Internal Promotion & Backlog Progression Rules
- **Verified Structure:** Students who fail or miss an End-Term Examination (or are detained due to attendance/UMC) accumulate a **Reappear / Backlog** in that course.
- **Year-to-Year Promotion Cutoffs:** **Not verified from an official public URL** — exact minimum CGPA (commonly benchmarked around passing CGPA thresholds per UGC/LPU programme ordinances, e.g., avoiding Academic Dismissal/Probation and meeting minimum credit accumulation) and maximum backlog limits for promotion to 2nd/3rd/4th year **vary by programme and admission batch**. Check your active Academic Ordinance under **UMS -> Academic Regulations**.
- **Mentor Support for At-Risk Students:** Division of Student Relationship (DSR) and assigned Faculty Mentors proactively track grades and schedule remedial tutorials and parent interactions for academically struggling students (`lpu.in/academics/mentoring-advising.php`).

## 2. Migration / Transfer Into LPU (Advanced Standing Policy — Verified)
Verified directly from `https://www.lpu.in/admission/migration.php`:
- **Eligibility:** Currently pursuing a regular degree at an accredited institution in a similar programme; meets batch eligibility %; maximum **1-year gap** permitted with Gap Certificate; must have at least **2 semesters of regular study remaining** at LPU.
- **No Backlog Condition:** Applicants having any **Backlog / Compartment / Reappear** in any subject may **not** be considered for migration. (Also generally not applicable to Integrated, Industry Collaborative, Work Integrated, or International Credit Transfer programmes; may be considered for Honours).
- **Scholarship Rule:** **Scholarship is NOT applicable** to migrating / Advanced Standing applicants.
- **Timeline:** Apply **up to December** for Even Term; **before the last date of admission** for Odd Term.
- **Fees:**
  - Registration/Processing Fee: **₹5,000** (non-refundable in all cases).
  - Migration/Transfer Fee (after Equivalence Committee approval, 10–15 working days): **₹5,000** + current term fee.
- **Contact:** `migration@lpu.co.in`, Division of Admissions, LPU Phagwara.

## 3. Outgoing Migration Certificate (Existing LPU Students Leaving/Graduating)
Apply online via **UMS / Official Certificate Portal** (`https://ums.lpu.in/lpuums/LoginNew.aspx?loginPage=ExtCert`) after completing no-dues clearance.
