# Attendance Policy at LPU

## Direct Answer
To be eligible to appear in the **End-Term Examination (ETE)** for a course at LPU, students are generally required to maintain a **minimum of 75% attendance in each individual course/subject** (with some programmes expecting 80% aggregate and providing a 5% relaxation buffer down to 75% for genuine exigencies or medical reasons). Falling below the minimum attendance cutoff can result in **detention** from the ETE for that course.

## Verification Notice
> While the **75% minimum attendance rule**, UMS real-time tracking, Duty Leave (DL), and detention consequences are consistently referenced in LPU academic guidance, **the full numeric LPU Academic Ordinance PDF is hosted behind the student UMS login rather than public `lpu.in` pages**. Exact medical-condonation ceilings, bonus CA attendance marks (if applicable to your batch/course), and statutory regulatory rules (e.g., PCI/BCI/COA/NCTE) must always be confirmed on **UMS -> Academic Regulations / E-Notice**.

## Detailed Attendance Rules & Concepts

### 1. How Attendance Is Calculated
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Attendance \%} = \frac{\text{Classes Attended} + \text{Approved Duty Leaves (DL)}}{\text{Total Classes Delivered to Date}} \times 100$$
- **Example:** If 40 classes have been delivered in a course and you attended 28 lectures plus 2 approved Duty Leaves (`30 / 40`), your effective attendance is **75.0%**.
- **Subject-wise vs Aggregate:** Eligibility is checked **per course** on UMS/LPU Touch (Theory and Practical/Lab components may be tracked individually depending on course credit structure). Even if your overall aggregate across 6 courses is 85%, having 68% in one specific course puts you at detention risk in *that* course.
- **Projections:** Use UMS's "Delivered / Attended" counts carefully: near the end of classes (e.g., December 11 in Autumn Term 2026), remaining lecture slots narrow quickly.

### 2. Duty Leaves (DL)
- **What qualifies (General Practice):** Official university representation (sports, cultural fests, hackathons, technical workshops, placement drives, NCC/NSS) or approved academic/institutional duty authorized by DSW, Division of Career Services, or the Head of School.
- **How to apply:** Duty Leave requests are submitted/verified via the designated event coordinator and tracked inside **UMS / RMS**. Always verify within a few days that the DL reflects in your UMS attendance register.

### 3. Medical Grounds & Condonation
- Severe hospitalization or medical emergencies must be reported promptly with valid medical records (often routed through **Uni Health Centre** verification / Academic Affairs / Mentor via UMS).
- Short illnesses are normally absorbed within the built-in 25% absence allowance (100% − 75%). Whether additional medical relaxation (e.g., below 75% down to 65%–70%) is permitted depends strictly on the Academic Ordinance of your programme and statutory council (for instance, Pharmacy/Law/Architecture/Education follow strict PCI/BCI/COA/NCTE attendance mandates). *Exact condonation limit for your batch: Verify via UMS Academic Ordinances.*

### 4. Consequences of Attendance Shortage (Detention)
- A student failing to meet the minimum attendance cutoff by the close of classes is marked **Detained** in that course, barred from writing the ETE for that subject, and awarded a detention/re-registration grade requiring the student to **repeat the course** (backlog clearance) in a subsequent eligible term or summer term if offered.
- Attendance shortages may also affect scholarship continuation rules.

### 5. Attendance Bonus Marks in CA
- **Status:** Partially verified / course-dependent. Some LPU courses award graded attendance marks within Continuous Assessment (e.g., 90%+ brackets) as specified in the **Course Instructional Plan (IP)** distributed at the start of the semester. Always check your course's IP on UMS/MyClass to confirm whether attendance carries CA weightage in that subject.

## Practical Student Checklist
1. Check **UMS / LPU Touch** weekly—flag faculty marking discrepancies immediately via RMS or to the course faculty within the correction window announced by the university.
2. Do not burn the 25% absence margin casually in August–September; keep it as a buffer for illness, travel, or mid-term prep.
3. Keep hard copies/PDFs of Uni Health Centre or hospital records if seriously ill.
