# Curriculum Structure, Outcome-Based Education & Minors

## Direct Answer
LPU's curriculum follows **Outcome-Based Education (OBE)** and the **Choice Based Credit System (CBCS)**. Programmes combine Foundation & Core Courses with **Engineering/Discipline Minors**, **Open/Interdisciplinary Minors** (70+ tracks across Computing, Business, Design, Law, Humanities, Sciences, Health, and Agriculture), **Industry Interface** (live projects, MOOCs, hackathons), and **Placement Pathways**.

## Verified B.Tech. CSE Curriculum Architecture
Verified directly from `https://www.lpu.in/programmes/engineering/btech-computer-science-and-engineering`:
- **Foundation & Core Courses** (Programming, Data Structures, Algorithms, OS, DBMS, Computer Networks, Software Engineering, etc.)
- **Engineering Minors & Specialisation Electives** (AI & ML, Data Science, Cloud Computing, Cyber Security, Full Stack, IoT, etc.)
- **Open Minor / Generic Elective** (4-course interdisciplinary sequence outside the core major)
- **Industry Interface & Applied Components:** Industry certifications, **MOOCs (NPTEL/SWAYAM/Coursera)**, Hackathons, Competitive coding events, Workshops, Guest Lectures, Educational Trips, Live Projects, Incubation Projects, and NGO/Community Projects.
- **Undergraduate Research & Study Abroad Pathways**
- **Placement Pathways:** Integrated Soft Skills, Verbal Ability, Analytical Skills, and Foreign Languages (`Centre for Professional Enhancement`).

## 70+ Official Interdisciplinary Minor Categories
Verified from `https://www.lpu.in/academics/Interdisciplinary-minors.php`:
- **Computing & Engineering:** Cyber Security, Data Science, ML/AI, IoT, Cloud Computing, Web/Mobile Development, CAD/CAM, Embedded Systems, EV/Robotics, etc.
- **Business, Finance & Law:** Financial Markets, Digital Marketing, Human Resource Management, Entrepreneurship, Business Analytics, Corporate/Cyber Law, Intellectual Property Rights.
- **Sciences, Agriculture & Health:** Biotechnology, Clinical Nutrition, Forensic Sciences, Organic Farming, Physiotherapy & Fitness.
- **Design, Media & Humanities:** Film & TV Production, UI/UX Design, Fashion, Photography, Journalism, Performing Arts, Foreign Languages.

> Note: Curriculum is periodically updated by Boards of Studies; exact syllabus, Course Outcomes (COs), and Instructional Plans (IPs) for your admission batch are accessed via **UMS** and the programme page syllabus viewer.
