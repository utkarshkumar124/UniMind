# LPU Academic System & Structure

## Direct Answer
Lovely Professional University follows a two-semester-per-year **Outcome-Based Choice Based Credit System (CBCS)**. An academic year is split into **Autumn Term (Term-I, late July to December)** and **Spring Term (Term-II, January to May)**. Every student is assigned a **Faculty Mentor**, and academic records (timetables, credits, attendance, CA, ETE, and grades) are centrally tracked on the **University Management System (UMS)**.

## Core Pillars of the Academic System
1. **Semester & Credit System:**
   - Regular programmes operate on a semester model (e.g., 8 semesters for B.Tech., 4 semesters for MBA, 6 semesters for BBA/BCA).
   - Courses carry assigned **credits** weighting Lecture (L), Tutorial (T), and Practical (P) hours. Exact total graduation credits depend on the programme and admission batch (`curriculum` section in UMS).
2. **Choice Based Credit System (CBCS):**
   - Students customize degree pathways using **Core Courses**, **Discipline Electives**, **Engineering/Discipline Minors**, **Open/Interdisciplinary Minors (4 cohesive courses)**, and **Generic Electives**.
3. **Mentoring & Remedial Academic Support:**
   - Every LPU student is paired with a dedicated **Faculty Mentor** (`lpu.in/academics/mentoring-advising.php`) who monitors attendance and academic progress, conducts one-on-one goal sessions, coordinates **Parent-Teacher Interactions**, and arranges **Additional Tutorial Classes, Remedial Sessions, and Self-Learning Materials** if a student struggles in a course.
4. **MOOCs, SWAYAM, NPTEL & Coursera Integration:**
   - LPU officially encourages MOOCs and industry certifications (NPTEL, SWAYAM, Coursera) with provision for **academic credit/content mapping** and hackathon-based evaluation credit as per current curriculum circulars.
5. **Cohorts-Based Academic Progression & Programme Orientation Course:**
   - Students are guided via a **Programme Orientation Course** in their initial term and grouped into career-goal **cohorts** (Corporate Jobs, Higher Studies/GATE, Government Exams, Entrepreneurship, R&D, Study Overseas).
