# Academic Schools & Disciplines at LPU

## Direct Answer
Lovely Professional University offers **150+ programmes** organized across **27 official academic disciplines/schools** spanning Engineering, Management (Mittal School of Business), Computer Applications, Law, Pharmacy, Agriculture, Architecture, Design, Media, Sciences, Education, Humanities, and Polytechnic.
