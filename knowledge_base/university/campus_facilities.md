# Campus Infrastructure & Facilities

## Direct Answer
The **Lovely Professional University (LPU)** campus in Phagwara, Punjab is an integrated residential university city featuring specialized academic laboratories, a 10,000+ sq.mt. **Central Library (Block-37)**, **UniMall** shopping and dining complex, **Uni-Auditorium**, **Uni-Polis**, **24x7 Uni Health Centre**, **Uni-Hotel**, banking & postal branches with a dedicated PIN code (144411), 24x7 campus fire & security infrastructure, and green mobility.

## Core Campus Facilities
### Academic & Research Spaces
- **Specialized Laboratories:** Google Lab, iMac Lab, E-Cell Logic Room, Vertical Milling Centre, Subsonic Wind Tunnel, Computerized Aeronautical Gas Turbine Test Rig, Central Instrumentation Facility, Advanced Manufacturing Lab, Structural Analysis Lab, Plant Biotechnology Lab, Space Centre, Hi-Tech Polyhouse, TV News Studio, Fashion Design & Pattern-Making Labs.
- **Libraries:** Central Library located in **Block-37** alongside 12 branch libraries.
- **Auditoriums & Conventions:** Uni-Auditorium, Uni-Polis, Metal Magna, open-air amphitheaters, and conference halls.

### Shopping, Dining & Commercial Services (UniMall & Kiosks)
- **UniMall:** Multi-storey air-conditioned campus complex housing departmental stores (including **WH Smith**), branded apparel, electronics, bookstores, bowling alley, gyms, and multi-cuisine food courts.
- **Food Brands & Dining Kiosks:** Café Coffee Day, Domino's Pizza, La Pino'z Pizza, Lovely Sweets, Archies, plus South Indian, North Indian, African, Bhutanese, and international food kiosks across academic and residential blocks.

### Banking, ATMs, Post Office & Courier
- **Campus Banks:** Partnerships with 5 major Indian banks, on-campus branches, 24/7 **e-Lobby**, and campus-wide **24/7 ATMs**.
- **Western Union:** Available on campus for international/domestic remittances.
- **Post & Courier Services:** On-campus Post Office (PIN **144411**) and express domestic/international courier counters for student parcels and official mail.

### Security & Fire Safety
- **Security Force:** 200+ (with 400+ residential security staff) full-time trained personnel including ex-servicemen and dedicated lady security staff for girls' hostels.
- **Surveillance & Access:** **7,400+ CCTV cameras** (`security.php` notes 7,200+ / 7,400+), **automated turnstile gates** at entry/exit points, biometric smart-card verification, and boom barriers.
- **Fire Station:** Full-fledged in-house campus Fire Station with fire truck and trained firefighters.

### Accessibility & Green Sustainability
- **Universal Accessibility:** Lifts in over **80% of campus buildings**, ramps and handrails in every block, wheelchairs and intra-campus mobility transport for persons with disabilities, and accessible restrooms in 80%+ of academic and residential blocks.
- **Sustainability:** **7.5 MW** solar + biogas renewable energy, 3 Sewage Treatment Plants (main **5 MLD** plant), 30+ rainwater harvesting bores, **50,000 trees** (200+ species), **100% LED lighting**, complete ban on single-use plastics, wet-waste vermicomposting, and campus electric rickshaws / solar carts / bicycles.
