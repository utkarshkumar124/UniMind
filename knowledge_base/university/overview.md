# University Overview: Lovely Professional University (LPU)

## Direct Answer
**Lovely Professional University (LPU)** is a multidisciplinary private university located on Jalandhar–Delhi G.T. Road, Phagwara, Punjab (India) – 144411, recognized by the **University Grants Commission (UGC)** under Sections **2(f)** and **12(B)** of the UGC Act, 1956, and accredited with **NAAC Grade A++** (score **3.68 out of 4.0**). Founded in 2005 under the Lovely International Trust (which started its first business & IT school in 2001), LPU serves over **35,000+ regular students** (including **3,000+ international students** from 50+ countries and 28 Indian states/UTs) across **150+ academic programmes** and **27 academic schools/disciplines**.

## Institutional History & Milestones
- **2001:** First business & IT school established in Phagwara, Punjab under Lovely International Trust (Founder Chairman: Late Sh. Baldev Raj Mittal).
- **2002:** Engineering institutes and Pharmacy (B.Pharm / M.Pharm) programmes started.
- **2003:** Law (LL.B, integrated Law) and Education programmes commenced.
- **2004:** Architecture (B.Arch / M.Arch Executive) commenced.
- **2005:** Formal establishment of **Lovely Professional University (LPU)**.
- **2006:** Recognized by the **University Grants Commission (UGC)**.
- **2007:** Launched Open & Distance Learning (ODL) programmes with UGC recognition.
- **2010:** Became a member of the **Association of Indian Universities (AIU)**.
- **2014:** Business school received accreditation from **ACBSP (USA)**.
- **2018:** First private university in India granted **ICAR** accreditation by NAEAB for Agricultural Sciences.
- **2019:** Hosted the Indian Science Congress in North India (inaugurated by PM Narendra Modi).
- **2021:** Launched UGC-entitled **LPU Online** programmes; 13 LPU students represented India at Tokyo Olympics 2020 (including Neeraj Chopra's Olympic Gold).
- **2023:** Awarded **NAAC Grade A++** with a **3.68 / 4.0** CGPA score.

## Accreditations, Statutory Approvals & Rankings
- **UGC Recognition:** Recognized under Section **2(f)** and Section **12(B)** of the UGC Act, 1956, plus **UGC-DEB** entitlement for Online and Distance programmes.
- **NAAC Accreditation:** Highest **A++** grade with score **3.68**.
- **NBA Accreditation (Washington Accord — Tier-I Engineering equivalence):** Accredited for **2024–25, 2025–26, and 2026–27 (valid up to 30/06/2027)** for:
  1. B.Tech. Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)
  2. B.Tech. Civil Engineering (CE)
  3. B.Tech. Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE)
  4. B.Tech. Mechanical Engineering (ME)
  5. Master of Business Administration (MBA)
- **ICAR (NAEAB):** B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture and 8 M.Sc. (Agri. & Hort.) programmes.
- **Statutory Regulatory Councils:**
  - **Bar Council of India (BCI):** 3-year LL.B. & 5-year integrated Law programmes.
  - **Council of Architecture (COA):** Diploma in Architectural Assistantship, B.Arch., M.Arch. (Executive).
  - **Pharmacy Council of India (PCI):** B.Pharm. & M.Pharm.
  - **NCTE:** B.Ed., integrated B.Ed.-M.Ed., ITEP (B.A. B.Ed., B.Sc. B.Ed., B.Com. B.Ed.), B.P.Ed., M.Ed., M.P.Ed.
  - **RCI:** Special Education & Clinical/Applied Psychology programmes.
  - **ITPI:** Bachelor of Planning & Master of Planning.
  - **AICTE Clarification:** Under the Hon'ble Supreme Court judgment (*Bharathidasan University & Anr. vs AICTE*, CWP 2056/1999), UGC-established universities do not require separate prior AICTE approval to offer technical programmes.
- **Global Memberships:** AIU, ACU (UK), IAU (UNESCO), IIE, AACSB Business Education Alliance, Institution of Engineers (India).

## Frequently Asked Questions
- **Is an LPU degree valid for government jobs, UPSC, GATE, and abroad education?**
  Yes. LPU is recognized by the **UGC** under Sections 2(f) and 12(B), is a member of **AIU**, holds **NAAC A++ (3.68)** accreditation, and holds **NBA (Washington Accord)** accreditation for core B.Tech. branches (CSE, CE, ECE, ME) and MBA through June 30, 2027.
