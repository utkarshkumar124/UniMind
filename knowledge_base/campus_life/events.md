# Campus Events, Fests & Student Media — LPU

## Direct Answer
LPU hosts **500+ annual campus events**, major national cultural/technical fests including **YouthVibe** (Global Open Fest), **Spectra** (Inter-school cultural extravaganza), **One India** (celebrating all 28 states and 8 UTs), **Kitab Utsav**, **TechSprint**, **Freshers Talent Search**, **I.I.M.U.N.**, and live concerts, all tracked via **LPU Happenings (`https://happenings.lpu.in`)**, **YTV**, and the **Lovely World** magazine.

## 1. Signature Campus Fests & Events
- **YouthVibe:** LPU's flagship mega cultural, technical, and youth festival (2026 featured live performance by Farhan Akhtar).
- **Spectra:** Annual grand intra-university diversity & cultural festival.
- **One India:** Cultural showcase of traditions, processions, and folk dances across India's 28 states & 8 Union Territories.
- **Technical & Academic Events:** **TechSprint**, **Effi-Cycle** (human-electric hybrid vehicle competition), **AIU National Esports Championship** (BGMI/Valorant titles), **Gavelled Moot Court Competition**, **BizConfluence**, **Kitab Utsav** (Department of Youth Capital), **I.I.M.U.N.**, and **National Space Day** (with ISRO SAC).
- **Celebrity Concerts & Talks:** Past concerts and Youth Talks include Diljit Dosanjh, Satinder Sartaaj, Amit Trivedi, Sunidhi Chauhan, Guru Randhawa, BPraak, Darshan Raval, Paradox, Javed Akhtar, Supreme Court Justice Rajesh Bindal, and Srikanth Bolla.

## 2. Campus Media & Updates
- **LPU Happenings:** `https://happenings.lpu.in` — official campus news & events portal.
- **YTV & Electronic Media (Student Welfare Wing):** Student-run video broadcasting, campus TV screens, Voice of LPU, podcasts; Enquiries: `01824-444234`, `artsandculture.dsw@lpu.co.in`.
- **Lovely World:** In-house monthly print & digital university magazine.
