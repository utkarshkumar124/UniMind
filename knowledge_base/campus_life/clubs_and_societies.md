# Student Clubs & Organizations — LPU

## Direct Answer
LPU has **350+ Student Organizations** across **18+ categories** (Tech/Coding, Robotics, Entrepreneurship, Debate/MUN, Literature, Cultural, Music/Dance, Sports/Esports, Law, Aviation, Social Service), with **10,000+ student members** running **500+ events annually**. Joining a student organization requires **no membership fee**, and 2nd-year+ students with **CGPA ≥ 7.0** can even apply through **UMS** to start a new organization.

## 1. Membership & Leadership Rules (Verified)
Verified directly from `https://www.lpu.in/campus-life/student-organisations.php`:
- **No Membership Fee:** Organizations cannot charge students a general membership fee to join (optional event-specific fundraising follows the organization's approved constitution).
- **Eligibility to Join:** Students from **2nd year onwards** can become members of **multiple organizations**, but can serve on the **Organization Board of ONLY ONE Student Organization**.
- **Starting a New Student Organization (Promoter Rules):**
  - Promoter must be from **2nd year onwards** with a minimum **CGPA of 7.0** (can promote only one organization per year).
  - Must have a **Faculty/Staff Facilitator** (rank not below Lecturer or equivalent).
  - Must align with campus discipline and strictly avoid political, semi-political, religious, or discriminatory agendas.

## 2. UMS Paths for Student Organizations
- **Read Manual & Policies:** `UMS Navigation -> Student Welfare System (SWS) -> Policies -> Department Wise -> Student Organization Cell -> Department of Student Organization Manual`
- **Register New Organization:** `UMS Navigation -> Student Welfare System (SWS) -> Student Organization Registration`
