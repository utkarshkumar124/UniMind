# Campus Rules & Student Code of Conduct — LPU

## Direct Answer
Lovely Professional University enforces a strict **zero-tolerance policy** against **ragging, alcohol, smoking, drugs/narcotics, weapons, sexual harassment, caste-based discrimination, and disciplinary misconduct**. Campus security monitors movement 24x7 via **7,400+ CCTV cameras**, **automated turnstiles**, **boom barriers**, and **ex-military / female security staff**. Single-use plastics are banned campus-wide.

## 1. Core Conduct Standards (Verified)
- **Drug-Free, Alcohol-Free & Smoke-Free Campus:** Zero tolerance toward possession, consumption, or distribution of alcohol, tobacco/cigarettes, vapes, or narcotic drugs anywhere on campus or inside hostels (`lpu.in/student-services/security.php`).
- **Zero Tolerance for Ragging:** Punishable under **UGC Anti-Ragging Regulations** and criminal law (`lpu.in/anti-ragging.php`).
- **Anti-Sexual Harassment & Respect for Diversity:** Governed by the **POSH Act 2013**, **UGC Regulations 2015**, and LPU's **Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)** and Anti-Caste Discrimination Committee.
- **Turnstile Gates & Boom Barriers:** Every student must carry their valid LPU ID card/smart card when entering or exiting campus gates, turnstiles, hostels, and the Block-37 Central Library.
- **Political & Religious Neutrality in Clubs:** Student organizations are prohibited from political, semi-political, or divisive religious activities or humiliating any community on the basis of race, caste, gender, religion, or disability.
- **Environmental Conduct:** Complete ban on **single-use plastics**; mandatory waste segregation in green (wet) and blue (dry) dustbins.
