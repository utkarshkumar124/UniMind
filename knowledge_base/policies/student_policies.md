# Student Administrative Procedures, Certificates & Policies

## Direct Answer
Official university certificates and academic documents—including **Bonafide Certificates, Character Certificates, Migration Certificates, Official Transcripts, Provisional Degree Certificates, and Duplicate Grade Sheets**—are requested online through LPU's official **UMS Certificate Portal (`https://ums.lpu.in/lpuums/LoginNew.aspx?loginPage=ExtCert`)** or via **UMS -> Relationship Management System (RMS)**.

## 1. How to Request Certificates, Transcripts & Degree Documents
1. Go to **`https://ums.lpu.in/lpuums/LoginNew.aspx?loginPage=ExtCert`** (linked directly on `https://www.lpu.in/` as *Apply Certificate*) or log in to **UMS** -> Academic/Student Services -> Certificate Request.
2. Select the document type needed (Bonafide, Fee Structure/Loan Letter, Transcript, Migration, Character, Provisional Degree, Final Degree Dispatch).
3. Complete any required **No-Dues Clearance** (Library Block-37, Hostel/Residential Services, Lab, Accounts/Fee Department).
4. Pay the applicable certificate/courier fee shown on the UMS screen and track status inside UMS (`Alumni` support also available via `alumni.lpu.in` / `alumnicentral@lpu.co.in` for passed-out graduates).

### Special Administrative Procedures
- **Education Loan Offer Letter / Fee Letter:** Download *Application Form for Loan Letter* or email Name & Application ID to `studentloanassistance@lpu.co.in` (`+91-1824-444583`).
- **Marks / Attendance Discrepancy:** Raise an RMS ticket in UMS (`ums.lpu.in`) and contact Division of Student Relationship (**Block 32, Room No. 100**, `01824-520150`, `dsr@lpu.co.in`) if unresolved.
- **Migration / Transfer Into LPU:** Submit Advanced Standing application and ₹5,000 processing fee via `migration@lpu.co.in` (`lpu.in/admission/migration.php`).
