# Grievance Redressal, Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) & Appeals — LPU

## Direct Answer
Students at LPU can lodge formal grievances through **UMS -> Relationship Management System (RMS)**, via the public OTP-verified **Student Grievance Redressal Form** (`https://www.lpu.in/student-grievance-redressal.php` covering Examination, Academic, Fees, and Other categories), or escalate to the **Student Grievance Redressal Committee (SGRC)** chaired by **Dr. Lovi Raj Gupta** and Ombudsperson **Dr. C.L. Chandan**. Sexual harassment complaints go directly to the **Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)** (`icc@lpu.co.in`, Presiding Officer **Neeta Raj**, `+91-8283921144`).

### Grievance Escalation Steps
1. **Level 1 (Routine Operational):** Log a ticket on **UMS -> RMS** or meet your Faculty Mentor / Division of Student Relationship (**Block 32, Room No. 100**, `+91-1824-520150`, `dsr@lpu.co.in`).
2. **Level 2 (Formal Grievance Portal):** Submit via **`lpu.in/student-grievance-redressal.php`** (Name, Email OTP verification, Phone, Nature of Grievance: *Examination / Academic / Fees / Others*, Description, Attachment).
3. **Level 3 (Statutory / Executive):** Escalate to the SGRC / Ombudsperson or Office of the Registrar (`registrar@lpu.co.in`, `+91-1824-510274`, `+91-9915020408`).
