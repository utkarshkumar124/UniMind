# Anti-Ragging Policy & Emergency Contacts — LPU

## Direct Answer
**Ragging is strictly prohibited** across Lovely Professional University—both on campus and off campus—as a **zero-tolerance criminal offence** governed by **UGC Regulations**. Any student found guilty of ragging or abetting ragging faces immediate punitive action (suspension, expulsion, FIR). For immediate help, contact LPU's **Anti-Ragging Committee (`+91-9876644331`)**, **Anti-Ragging Squad (`+91-9878426874`)**, or the **24x7 National Anti-Ragging Helpline (`1800-180-5522`)**.
