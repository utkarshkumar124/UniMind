# Disciplinary Rules & Fine Framework — LPU

## Direct Answer
LPU enforces strict **zero-tolerance disciplinary standards** covering **ragging, alcohol, smoking/vaping, drugs/narcotics, weapons, exam Unfair Means (UMC), sexual harassment, unauthorized room swapping/appliances in hostels, and single-use plastics**. Security personnel monitor campus 24x7 via **7,400+ CCTVs** and biometric turnstiles, escalating violations to the **Disciplinary Committee** or **UMC Committee**.

## 1. Zero-Tolerance & Strictly Prohibited Acts (Verified)
- **Narcotics, Alcohol & Smoking:** Strictly banned inside hostels and anywhere on campus; security checks operate 24x7 (`lpu.in/student-services/security.php`).
- **Ragging & Bullying:** Criminal offence under UGC Regulations (`lpu.in/anti-ragging.php`).
- **Examination Unfair Means (UMC):** Carrying phones/smartwatches/chits into exam rooms or cheating causes paper cancellation, `'F'` grade, potential semester debarment, and scholarship loss.
- **Unauthorized Hostel Appliances / Room Changes:** Only approved appliances (**kettle, iron, trimmer, hair dryer, hair straightener**; **induction plate strictly in Apartments only**) are permitted; unauthorized room swapping is banned.
- **Club Misconduct:** Student organizations cannot conduct political, semi-political, religious, or discriminatory activities (`lpu.in/campus-life/student-organisations.php`).

## 2. Specific Fine Amounts Note
- Exact rupee fine slabs (e.g., for lost ID card replacement, unauthorized high-wattage hostel gadgets, property damage, late library return, or traffic violations inside campus) are maintained in **UMS -> Policies / Hostel Handbook / Library Rules** and are **Not verified from public `lpu.in` pages**. Check UMS Student Welfare System (SWS) -> Policies before contesting or paying a disciplinary fine.
