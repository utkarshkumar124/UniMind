# Reappear Examinations, Backlog Clearance & Improvement

## Direct Answer
If you fail a course or receive a **Reappear / Backlog** grade (`E` / `F` / `G`), you must apply online through **UMS -> Examination -> Reappear Registration** within the announced window and pay the prescribed reappear examination fee. For **Autumn Term (Term-I) 2026–2027**, the official **Last Date to Apply for Reappear** is **Saturday, August 29, 2026**, and **Reappear Examinations commence on Saturday, September 05, 2026**.

## 1. Types of Backlogs / Reappear Situations
1. **End-Term Reappear (Eligible Attendance Already Met):**
   - If you met attendance eligibility and earned valid CA/MTT marks but failed or were absent in the ETE, you register for a **Reappear Exam** in that subject during the scheduled reappear window.
2. **Detained / CA Shortage / Course Repeat Backlog:**
   - If you were **detained due to <75% attendance** or failed the overall course requiring re-study (commonly called an **'F' / Backlog repeat**), you may need to **re-register for the full course** (attending classes / summer term / backlog registration slot on UMS) rather than sitting only for the ETE. Check your exact grade code on UMS Result!

## 2. Verified 2026–2027 Reappear Schedule (Autumn Term Regular)
- **Last Date to Apply for Reappear (Term-I 2026–27):** **Saturday, August 29, 2026**
- **Reappear Examinations Start (Term-I 2026–27):** **Saturday, September 05, 2026**
- Similar reappear windows are announced for **Spring Term (Term-II)** and **Summer Term** via **UMS E-Notice**.

## 3. How to Apply for a Reappear Exam (Step-by-Step)
1. Log in to **UMS (`ums.lpu.in`)** or **LPU Touch**.
2. Navigate to **Examination / Reappear Registration** during the active E-Notice window.
3. Select the eligible pending backlog course(s) shown for your Registration Number.
4. Pay the applicable **reappear examination fee** online through UMS payment gateway and download the receipt/confirmation.
   > **Fee Verification Caveat:** Public `lpu.in` pages list the regular semester Examination Fee as **₹5,000 per semester** (for 2026 UG/PG admission programmes), but the **exact per-paper reappear / backlog / summer-term fee** is announced inside UMS E-Notices per academic session and is **Not verified from an official public page** — verify the live fee shown on UMS before paying.
5. Download your Reappear **Admit Card / Seating Plan** from UMS before the exam date.

## 4. Grade Improvement Examinations
- Students seeking to improve a passing grade in completed courses may be permitted **Grade Improvement** attempts subject to programme-specific Examination Ordinance caps (such as maximum credit/course limits, time limit after term, and whether higher/latest grade counts). **Verify exact Improvement eligibility and form dates in UMS -> E-Notice -> Examination.**
