# Results Declaration, Answer Book Scrutiny & Appeals

## Direct Answer
End-Term Examination (ETE) results are published directly inside **UMS (`ums.lpu.in`) -> Examination / View Marks & Grades**. For Autumn Term 2026–2027 Regular Programmes, results are scheduled on **Tuesday, January 12, 2027**, **Physical Scrutiny of ETE Answer Booklets** on **January 14 & January 16, 2027**, and **Online Grade Sheets** on **Wednesday, January 27, 2027**. If your marks look incorrect, LPU officially provides **physical answer-booklet scrutiny**, **online UMS re-evaluation**, and **RMS/Examination grievance redressal**.

## 1. How to Check Results & Grade Sheets
1. Log in to **UMS (`ums.lpu.in`)** or the **LPU Touch** mobile app.
2. Open **Examination -> Result / Grade Sheet / Provisional Marks** to view subject-wise grades, TGPA, and CGPA.
3. Keep UMS dues cleared: administrative/fee holds can temporarily restrict result/admit-card visibility on UMS.

## 2. Verified Result & Scrutiny Dates (Autumn Term 2026–2027)
- **ETE Examinations:** Dec 14 – Dec 30, 2026
- **Publication of Results:** **Tuesday, January 12, 2027**
- **Physical Scrutiny of ETE Answer Booklets:** **January 14 & January 16, 2027**
- **Online Grade Sheet Available:** **Wednesday, January 27, 2027**

## 3. What to Do If Your Marks Are Incorrect (CA, MTT, or ETE)
Verified from `https://www.lpu.in/academics/assessment-and-evaluation.php`:
1. **Incorrect Continuous Assessment (CA) Marks:**
   - Faculty share CA evaluations before ETE. Immediately contact your **subject teacher / mentor** during class/remedial hours AND log an academic request via **UMS -> Relationship Management System (RMS)** before CA marks freeze on UMS.
2. **Mid-Term Test (MTT / MTE) Discrepancy:**
   - Attend the **physical scrutiny** window announced by the Division of Examination to inspect your checked MTT booklet and report any totalling error or un-checked question on the spot.
3. **End-Term Examination (ETE) Discrepancy — Physical Scrutiny & Re-evaluation:**
   - **Step 1 — Physical Scrutiny (Jan 14 & 16, 2027 for Term-I):** Apply via UMS during the scrutiny schedule to physically inspect your ETE answer booklet. Totalling mistakes or unmarked answers can be flagged directly.
   - **Step 2 — Online Re-evaluation:** Apply online through **UMS -> Examination -> Re-evaluation** within the official UMS deadline if you seek re-checking of evaluated answers (subject to applicable UMS re-evaluation fee and rules posted per term E-Notice).
4. **Escalation:** If unresolved, file an **Examination Grievance** via UMS RMS or the official **Student Grievance Redressal** portal (`https://www.lpu.in/student-grievance-redressal.php`).
