# LPU Evaluation & Assessment System

## Direct Answer
Lovely Professional University uses a continuous formative and summative assessment system consisting of **Continuous Assessment (CA)** (quizzes, assignments, projects, practicals, hackathons, attendance where specified), **Mid-Term Tests (MTT / MTE)**, and **End-Term Examinations (ETE)**. Evaluators check booklets systematically using **table marking**, and students can physically inspect their evaluated MTT/ETE answer booklets through **scrutiny** before or after results.

## 1. Evaluation Components
1. **Continuous Assessment (CA):**
   - Conducted throughout the semester via multiple scheduled components listed in the course's **Instructional Plan (IP)** (available on UMS/MyClass).
   - Components may include written class tests, objective quizzes, term papers, case studies, live/community projects, hackathons/competitive coding, lab/practical evaluations, and in some courses, graded attendance.
   - Skill-Based CA (`lpu.in/academics/curriculum-innovations.php`) integrates hands-on application, mock interviews, and hackathon challenges.
2. **Mid-Term Test (MTT / MTE):**
   - Formal scheduled mid-semester examination conducted mid-term (**Oct 1 – Oct 9, 2026** for Autumn Term 2026–27).
   - Evaluates the first half of the course syllabus under supervised exam conditions.
3. **End-Term Examination (ETE):**
   - Comprehensive end-of-semester examination (**Dec 14 – Dec 30, 2026** for Autumn Term 2026–27) covering the full syllabus.
   - Practical ETE / viva-voce / project jury is conducted separately for laboratory and studio/project courses.

## 2. Weightage & Component Differences by Course Type
> **Important Verification Note:** Exact percentage splits between CA, MTT, and ETE **are NOT identical across every LPU course**. Theory lecture courses, practical/lab-only courses, capstone projects, architecture studios, and pharmacy/law courses have distinct evaluation schemes defined in each course's **Course Syllabus & Instructional Plan** on **UMS**. Typical engineering theory courses commonly balance CA + MTT + ETE with ETE carrying major weight (often ~50%), while practical courses weight continuous lab performance and end-term practicals — *always verify your specific subject's weightage in UMS -> Instructional Plan / Syllabus*.

## 3. Quality Assurance in Grading
- Verified from `lpu.in/academics/assessment-and-evaluation.php`:
  - **Table Marking:** Question-wise / structured answer-book evaluation in dedicated faculty evaluation zones.
  - **Physical Scrutiny:** Students can view their evaluated MTT and ETE answer booklets in person to check totalling errors or ungraded answers.
  - **Re-evaluation:** Transparent online re-evaluation application via UMS if a student disagrees with marks after scrutiny.
