# Examination Rules & Unfair Means (UMC) Policy

## Direct Answer
To sit for an LPU **End-Term Examination (ETE)**, a student must meet the **attendance eligibility requirement (typically 75% per course)**, hold a valid **Admit Card / Student ID** on UMS, and follow strict exam hall conduct. Possessing **mobile phones, smartwatches, chits, or unauthorized material** inside the exam hall — even switched off — triggers an **Unfair Means Case (UMC)** with severe academic penalties including paper cancellation, `'F'` grade, debarment, and loss of scholarship eligibility.

## 1. Examination Eligibility Requirements
1. **Attendance Threshold:** Minimum course-wise attendance requirement (generally 75%) satisfied before classes close.
2. **Admit Card & Seating Plan:** Downloaded/checked via **UMS / LPU Touch** prior to MTT and ETE. Seating block, room number, and reporting time are published inside UMS before every exam slot.
3. **Fee / No-Dues Clearance:** Tuition and examination fee obligations cleared for the term (`indianexaminationfee`: **₹5,000 per semester** for regular 2026 intake UG/PG programmes checked).
4. **Permitted Calculator / Material Rules:** Only non-programmable calculators (if permitted for that course paper), valid physical/digital University ID card as notified, and standard stationery.

## 2. Unfair Means Case (UMC) — Prohibited Conduct & Consequences
LPU enforces a strict examination integrity policy (`lpu.in` & UMS Examination Guidelines):
- **Triggers for UMC Booking:**
  - Carrying a **mobile phone, smartwatch, Bluetooth earpiece, programmable calculator**, or any electronic communication device into the exam room (even if switched off).
  - Possessing handwritten/printed notes, chits, writings on hands/desk/admit card/scale, or textbooks inside the hall.
  - Talking, copying, exchanging answer sheets/question papers, or impersonation.
  - Refusing to surrender suspicious material to the invigilator or misbehaving with examination staff.
- **Process & Penalties:**
  - Invigilator confiscates the material, records an **Unfair Means Case (UMC)** report, and summons the student before the **UMC Committee** for a formal hearing.
  - Verified consequences include **cancellation of the concerned examination paper / award of `'F'` grade**, **debarment from one or more examinations/semesters** for severe/repeat offences, formal disciplinary notation, and **revocation of LPU merit scholarship**.
  - **Specific UMC Category Table:** Exact category-wise punishment tiers are defined inside the **LPU Examination Guidelines on UMS** (not published publicly without login).
