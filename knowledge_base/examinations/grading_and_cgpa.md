# Grading System, TGPA, CGPA Calculation & Passing Criteria

## Direct Answer
LPU calculates semester **TGPA (Term Grade Point Average)** and cumulative **CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average)** on a **10-point credit-weighted scale**: multiply each course's **credits** by the **grade points** earned, sum them, and divide by total **credits registered/considered**. LPU uses a mix of **relative and absolute grading** depending on course enrolment size and programme ordinances.

## Verification Notice
> Detailed grade-point tables (`O` = 10 down to `F` = 0, reappear/detained/incomplete codes `E`/`F`/`G`/`I`) and passing-mark minimums (commonly 40% aggregate and minimum ETE score in many regular courses, or 50% in regulated PG/PCI/COA/BCI programmes) are governed by batch-specific **LPU Examination Ordinances** accessible inside **UMS**. Do not assume one fixed cutoff applies to every school; check **UMS -> Result / Grade Sheet / Academic Ordinance** for your batch's official scale.

## 1. How to Calculate TGPA and CGPA

### Step-by-Step Formula
1. **Term Grade Point Average (TGPA / SGPA):**
   $$\text{TGPA} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} (C_i \times GP_i)}{\sum_{i=1}^{n} C_i}$$
   where $C_i$ = credit value of course $i$, and $GP_i$ = grade point awarded in course $i$.

2. **Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA):**
   $$\text{CGPA} = \frac{\sum_{\text{all valid courses}} (C_j \times GP_j)}{\sum_{\text{all valid courses}} C_j}$$
   When you clear a **reappear/backlog** course, the updated passing grade replaces the earlier failing grade in the CGPA calculation as per the applicable examination ordinance.

### Worked Practical Example (1 Semester)
Suppose a student takes 4 courses in Term-I:
| Course | Credits ($C_i$) | Grade Point ($GP_i$) | Weighted Points ($C_i \times GP_i$) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Data Structures | 4 | 9 (`A+`) | $4 \times 9 = 36$ |
| Discrete Mathematics | 4 | 8 (`A`) | $4 \times 8 = 32$ |
| Digital Electronics | 3 | 7 (`B+`) | $3 \times 7 = 21$ |
| Python Lab | 2 | 10 (`O`) | $2 \times 10 = 20$ |
| **Total** | **13 Credits** | — | **109 Weighted Points** |

$$\text{TGPA} = \frac{109}{13} = 8.38$$

If in Term-II the student earns **120 weighted points** across **15 credits** ($\text{TGPA} = 8.00$), their cumulative CGPA is:
$$\text{CGPA} = \frac{109 + 120}{13 + 15} = \frac{229}{28} = 8.18$$

## 2. Relative vs Absolute Grading
- In larger multi-section courses, LPU frequently applies **statistical/relative grading**, meaning letter grade cutoffs (`O`, `A+`, `A`, `B+`, `B`, `C`, `P`, `E`/`F`) depend on class mean and standard deviation rather than fixed percentage boundaries alone.
- In small-cohort or specialized studio/project courses, **absolute grading** thresholds apply.

## 3. Passing Criteria & CGPA-to-Percentage Conversion
- **Passing Criteria:** To pass a regular course, a student typically must meet both the minimum overall percentage/grade threshold AND minimum ETE/end-term component threshold specified for their programme. Statutory council programmes (B.Pharm., Law, B.Arch., M.Tech., etc.) can mandate higher minimum passing marks (often 50%). **Verify exact passing threshold on your UMS Instructional Plan / Ordinance.**
- **CGPA to Percentage Conversion:** Standard LPU conversion factors (commonly `CGPA × 10` on official transcripts/declarations for many batches, or older `CGPA × 9.5` for legacy schemes) must be verified from the official **Conversion Formula printed on the back of your LPU Grade Sheet / Transcript** or requested via UMS RMS/Certificate cell — **do not guess** when filling job or higher-study forms.
