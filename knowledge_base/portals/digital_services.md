# Digital Services & IT Infrastructure — LPU

## Direct Answer
LPU's campus digital ecosystem runs on an enterprise campus network of **55+ in-house servers, 70 km of fiber-optic cable, 25 Gbps bandwidth, and 3,000+ Wi-Fi access points**, powering LPU's flagship ERP **University Management System (UMS)**, the **LPU Touch** mobile app, **MyClass** virtual classrooms, **LPU Live** communication app, **Relationship Management System (RMS)**, **Pro Bricks** (residential/mess/laundry/gym app), and **KOHA / Mapmyaccess** library systems.

## 1. UMS Core Student Modules (Verified)
Verified from `https://www.lpu.in/student-services/ums.php`:
- **LMS (Learning Management System):** Syllabus, Instructional Plans (IPs), lecture slides, assignments, and uploaded notes.
- **RMS (Relationship Management System):** Central digital ticketing desk for academic queries, attendance correction escalations, certificates, fee/hostel issues, and departmental follow-ups.
- **Examination Module:** Exam seating plans, admit cards, CA/MTT/ETE marks, re-evaluation, scrutiny booking, reappear registration, and Grade Sheets.
- **Attendance Module:** Real-time subject-wise attendance percentage and Duty Leave (DL) updates.
- **Courses & Timetable:** Personal weekly timetable, elective/Open Minor registration, faculty details.
- **E-Notice Board:** Authoritative university circulars on exams, fees, holidays, library hours, and hostel rules.
- **Fee Details:** Online semester, exam, reappear, and hostel/transport fee payments and receipts.
- **Placements, Library, Residential Service & Transportation:** End-to-end campus service portals inside UMS.

## 2. Mobile Companion Apps
- **LPU Touch** (`Google Play: ums.lovely.university`): Official mobile app for UMS timetable, attendance, announcements, results, RMS, and gate passes.
- **Pro Bricks:** Companion residential app managing hostel rooms, mess menus/rebates, laundry, and gym access (`lpu.in/student-services/residence.php`).

> **Security Note:** Never share your UMS registration ID/password on third-party sites. Official LPU domains always end in `.lpu.in` (plus `lpuonline.com` / `lpude.in` / `lpu.co.in` email).
