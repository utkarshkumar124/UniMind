# Fee Refund Policy & Withdrawal Guidelines — LPU

## Direct Answer
Admission cancellation and refund deductions at LPU depend strictly on the **date the refund application is submitted**, tracked online through **LPUAdmit (`https://admission.lpu.in/`) -> Post Admission Services**. Charges like Prospectus, Application, Processing (e.g., ₹5,000 migration processing), Entrance/LPUNEST, Late, and Programme Transfer fees are **strictly non-refundable**, and LPU pays **no interest** on refunded amounts.

## 2. Key Non-Refundable & Loan Refund Rules
- **Non-Refundable Fees:** Prospectus fee, Application fee, Entrance (LPUNEST) fee, Processing fee (e.g., Migration processing ₹5,000), Late fee, Programme Transfer fee, and Migration fee are **non-refundable under all circumstances**.
- **Education Loan Refunds:** If fees were paid directly via a bank Education Loan, the refund is normally issued via an **account-payee cheque favoring the sanctioning bank**, unless the student submits a valid **No Objection Certificate (NOC)** from the bank.
- **No Interest:** Zero interest is paid on deposited or refunded amounts.

## 3. How to Apply & Track a Fee Refund
1. Log in to **LPUAdmit (`https://admission.lpu.in/`)**.
2. Download/submit the **Refund Application Form** under **Post Admission Services** (or consult your assigned Admission Counsellor / `+91-1824-517000`).
3. Track real-time processing status under **LPUAdmit -> Post Admission Services**.
