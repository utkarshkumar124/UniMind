# Fee Structure (Session 2026–2027) — LPU

## Direct Answer
For the **2026 intake**, LPU's official programme fees (before scholarships) for flagship regular programmes are **₹1,60,000 per semester** for **B.Tech. CSE (`P132`)**, **₹2,20,000 per semester** for **MBA (`P371-NNAF`)**, and **₹80,000 per semester** for **BBA (`P321`)**, plus an **Examination Fee of ₹5,000 per semester**. Annual **Residential Hostel Fees (2026–2027)** range from **₹72,000 to ₹1,80,000**, and **Transport Fees (2026–2027)** range from **₹25,000 to ₹70,000** depending on route and AC/Non-AC option.

> **Important Scope Note:** Programme tuition fees vary across disciplines (Engineering vs Management vs Sciences vs Law vs Design), specialized/honours/industry-collaborative tracks, and admission cohorts. Because earlier batches (e.g., 2023/2024/2025) may carry different locked semester fees, always verify your **personal payable fee ledger inside UMS (`ums.lpu.in -> Fee Details`)** or check any other programme's live 2026 fee on `https://www.lpu.in/programmes/`.

### International Applicants (2026 B.Tech. CSE Reference)
- Standard International Tuition (Phases 1–3): **USD 2,950 / semester** (Phase 4: USD 2,750; Phase 5: USD 2,450); Sri Lanka / Nepal / Bhutan / Bangladesh special bracket (`Phase 1–4`: ₹1,90,000 / sem); International Examination Fee: **USD 75 / sem** (SAARC ₹4,500).

## 2. Residential & Mess Charges (2026–2027 Summary)
- **Standard Air-Cooled Hostels:** ₹72,000 (5/6/7/8/4x2-str) to ₹1,10,700 (2-str) per year.
- **Standard AC Hostels:** ₹81,000 (5/6/7/8-str) to ₹1,46,700 (1-str) per year.
- **Studio Apartments AC:** ₹99,000 (4-str) to ₹1,80,000 (1-str) per year.
- **Mess Fee:** Billed separately inside UMS/LPUAdmit (*exact 2026–27 mess slab not listed in public `residence.php` table*).

## 3. Transport Charges (2026–2027 Summary)
- **Non-AC Bus Routes:** ₹25,000–₹70,000/year across Jalandhar, Adampur, Kapurthala, Hoshiarpur, Nawanshahr, Ludhiana, Amritsar, and Mukerian.
- **AC Bus (Ludhiana Route):** ₹35,000–₹70,000/year.

## 4. Migration / Advanced Standing Fees (2026)
- Processing Fee (Non-refundable): **₹5,000**; Migration/Transfer Fee on approval: **₹5,000** (`lpu.in/admission/migration.php`).
