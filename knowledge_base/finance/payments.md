# Fee Payments, Deadlines & Late Payment Rules

## Direct Answer
Existing LPU students pay semester tuition, examination, residential, and transport fees online through **UMS (`ums.lpu.in`) -> Fee Details / Online Payment** (or via **LPU Touch** / **LPUAdmit** for new admissions). Under LPU's official fee rule (`GetProgramFeeDetail`), **the fee for each successive semester must be paid before the teaching period of the preceding semester ends** (for example, by early December 2026 ahead of Spring Term-II 2027).

## 1. How to Pay Fees at LPU
1. **Continuing Students (`UMS` & `LPU Touch`):**
   - Log in to **UMS (`ums.lpu.in`)** using Registration Number and Password.
   - Open **Fee Details / Pay Fee Online** to view pending semester tuition, examination fee (`₹5,000/sem` for 2026 regular cohorts), hostel/electricity dues, or reappear charges.
   - Pay via **UPI, Net Banking, Credit/Debit Card**, or sanctioned bank/education-loan NEFT/RTGS challan uploaded per UMS instructions.
2. **New Applicants (`LPUAdmit`):**
   - Pay initial registration/admission/hostel booking fees inside **`https://admission.lpu.in/`**.
3. **1st-Semester Examination Fee Rule (2026 Verified):**
   - Per official fee comments, **the 1st-semester examination fee (₹5,000) can be paid along with the 2nd-semester fee or earlier**.

## 2. Successive Semester Payment Deadline
- Official LPU Fee Term: **"The fee for each successive semester shall be payable before the preceding semester’s teaching period ends."**
- For **Autumn Term 2026–27** (where classes close **December 11, 2026**), students should check **UMS -> Fee Details / E-Notice** in November/early December for the exact cutoff date without late charges.

## 3. Installments & Late Payment Rules
- **Installments / Loan Timing:** Standard semester fees are payable per semester before term cutoffs. Students financing via bank/NBFC loans or **Easy EMI partners** coordinate disbursements through `studentloanassistance@lpu.co.in`. Short-term installment extensions, where granted, require formal UMS fee-extension approval.
- **Late Payment Fines:** Exact slab-wise late payment penalty amounts are published dynamically in each semester's **UMS Fee Schedule E-Notice** (**Not verified as a fixed universal amount on public `lpu.in` pages**). Unpaid dues can place a hold on UMS course registration, admit card download, or result viewing.
