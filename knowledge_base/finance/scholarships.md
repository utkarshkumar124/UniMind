# Scholarships, Financial Aid & Education Loans — LPU

## Direct Answer
Over **7 out of 10 LPU students** receive scholarships at admission, with **₹100 Crore+** disbursed last year and benefits up to **₹6.8 Lakh** (plus **LPU RISE** offering **100% programme fee + 4-seater non-AC hostel waiver** for top national achievers). Scholarships are awarded across **Brackets 1–4** based on qualifying marks (10th, 12th, Graduation), **LPUNEST** categories (50% / 40% / 30% in B.Tech CSE Phase V), national tests (JEE Main, NEET, CUET, CAT, GATE, UCEED), Sports/Cultural/R&D achievements, and Financial Aid schemes.

## 1. Official Scholarship Brackets (Qualifying Exam Marks)
Verified from `https://www.lpu.in/scholarship/scholarship.php`:

| Scholarship Bracket | After 10th / 10+2 / Diploma / Lateral Entry | After Graduation Programmes (PG) |
|---|---|---|
| **Bracket 1** | **98% or above** in qualifying exam | **95% or above** in qualifying exam |
| **Bracket 2** | **95% – 97.99%** in qualifying exam | **90% – 94.99%** in qualifying exam |
| **Bracket 3** | **90% – 94.99%** in qualifying exam | **80% – 89.99%** in qualifying exam |

> *Aggregate calculation considers the best 5 subjects with highest marks in 10+2/qualifying exam, mandatorily including any programme-specific eligibility subjects (e.g., Physics, Maths for B.Tech).*

## 2. LPUNEST & Admission Phase Rules (2026 Verified)
- **LPUNEST Categories (B.Tech. CSE Reference):** Category I (`50%` of Programme Fee), Category II (`40%`), Category III (`30%`), Category IV (`30%` / varies by phase).
- **2026 Admission Phases:** Phase-I generally offers highest scholarship amounts (**till April 25, 2026**), Phase-II (**till June 2**), Phase-III (**till June 30**), Phase-IV (**till July 31**), Phase-V (**Aug 1 onwards**) — percentages vary slightly across programmes/phases.
- **LPU RISE (Recognising India's Scholarly Excellence):** **100% scholarship on Full Programme Fee + 4-Seater Non-AC Hostel accommodation** for top-tier national rankers/merit holders.

## 4. Continuation Rule (Important!)
- Scholarships awarded at admission generally apply across subsequent semesters **only if you meet continuation rules** (including passing courses without UMC / serious disciplinary action — UMC explicitly risks scholarship revocation). Confirm specific CGPA/promotion retention terms in your Admission Offer Letter / Prospectus Part-C.
- **Migrating / Advanced Standing Students:** Scholarships are **NOT** applicable.
